SunPoint Beacon

Clear insight.
From connection to report.

Choose the view that fits your needs, from a simple Home User grade to detailed engineering measurements.

01

Beacon Standard

Wired and Wi-Fi monitoring, connection grades, event history and reports. Use the local dashboard on your network and pair with Beacon Cloud for remote access.

02

Beacon+

All Standard monitoring, plus PiSugar battery backup and AC-power telemetry. Keep observing through a wall-power interruption and capture power-loss and recovery context.

03

Beacon Hub

Bring reporting Beacons together on a local collector. Review the fleet, apply configuration profiles and expose summary and per-Beacon measurements through read-only SNMP v2c or v3.

During an Internet or router outage, remote access may be unavailable. Beacon buffers supported reporting data and resumes uploads when connectivity returns. Battery runtime varies with load and conditions.

Your dashboard

01

Views for the task

Home User, Remote Work, Gaming and Business views emphasize the measurements relevant to each audience. ISP/MSP and Engineer views retain deeper technical detail.

02

Explanations beside the grade

See which current measurements are affecting the score. Enter optional expected Wired and Wi-Fi speeds so a consistently slow connection does not hide behind a learned baseline.

03

Reports you can use

Export PDF or CSV reports, or an AI-ready package with suggested prompts. Audience-specific wording helps you share the evidence with the right level of detail.

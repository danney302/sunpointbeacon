About SunPoint Beacon

Built on monitoring experience.
Focused on everyday reliability.

SunPoint Beacon is a product of SunPoint Global, combining practical network monitoring with clear, useful explanations.

The connection people actually use.

Availability alone does not explain buffering, unstable calls or a difficult gaming session. Beacon observes the connection from its own location and keeps the evidence needed to understand recurring problems.

One set of measurements. Useful perspectives.

Home users can start with a grade. Support teams can examine response time, packet loss, speed and event history. Hosts and property managers can review locations remotely, while technical teams can collect reporting devices through a Hub.

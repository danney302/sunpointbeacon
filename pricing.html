Prices are in USD per physical Beacon. Hardware is purchased separately. Applicable tax is additional. A directly connected Beacon and the same Beacon reporting through a Hub share one subscription place when their identity is confirmed. There is no extra Hub Cloud fee.

30 days to explore it all.

Eligible new activations include all available Cloud dashboards, reports and management features for 30 days. The trial starts when you activate Cloud, so shipping time does not use it up.

A credit card is required. Your chosen plan renews automatically after the trial at the price and billing frequency you select. Stripe shows the amount and first charge date before you confirm. Cancel in Settings → Subscription before the trial ends to avoid payment.

Included with every plan

Remote dashboards, 30-day history, PDF and CSV reports, available signed software updates, and email support. Each export covers up to seven days.

Support is provided through messaging. Live calls and support sessions are not included. Local monitoring, local dashboards and local reports continue without a Cloud subscription.

The public read-only live demo is separate and does not require a card.
